## Synthesis

- **Capability is not the issue.** When present, Irish dials at/above the connect goal and her conversation quality and qualified-lead numbers *improved* through the spring.
- **Output and consistency are the issue.** The number that pays the bills — **appointments set** — stayed at ~1.8/day and then **collapsed to 0.2/day in May**, alongside a drop to only **5 logged days** that month. This is consistent with the reported power-outage / availability problems.
- **Strategic context:** the team is moving off cold calling toward purchased/inbound (PPL) leads with acquisitions-led follow-up, which reduces the need for a dedicated cold-call/appointment-setter seat regardless of individual performance.

## Caveats (read before deciding)

- Figures are **logged days only**. Blank days may be days off, outages, or untracked work — so day-counts are *directional*, not a clock-in record.
- May is a partial month and may understate or overstate the trend.
- "Targets" shown are the scorecard goals; some (e.g., 150 connects, 200 dials) are aggressive bars.
