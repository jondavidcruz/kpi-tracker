Infill Lots — John's System

Vacant city lots → builders. Markets: Nashville · Houston · Atlanta (+ Charlotte Phase 2)
1

Count sold lots

Zillow: vacant lots SOLD in 30 days at $50k+ per zip. More sold = more builder demand.

2

Satellite check

Look for the house–lot–house pattern: scattered empty lots inside built-out streets.

3

New-build count

Homes built this year per zip = builder activity. The ONLY reliable TX signal (TX hides sold prices).

4

Price from the buyer

Offer = buyer's box price − your fee − closing. Start from the builder, never the seller.

Recreational Land — Hunter's System

Rural parcels → consumers. Markets: Hot Springs AR · Cherokee Village AR · Eufaula OK
1

Pick the city you SELL to

Buy 1–3 hrs out (one county over ≈ 1 hr). <1hr too pricey, >3hr too few buyers.

2

Call the county — 4 Qs

Camping limit? Min build size? Mobile homes OK? STR OK? No zoning dept = auto-green.

3

Satellite tell

RVs parked on bare land = camping allowed + no HOA in practice. Instant validator.

4

Sanity check price

Sell-through near 1:1, Zillow floor under $50k. Low-income county next to rich metro = the spread.

🧲 Direct REI Pull Filters

Vacant · ≤5.1 ac Assessed ≤ $10k Out-of-county / out-of-state owner Outside city limits Slope: 50%+ under 15% Not landlocked FEMA ≤ 50% · Wetlands ≤ 50% Exclude church/cemetery/LLC owners

🧮 The Offer Rule — ALL 3 must pass

① ≤ assessed value ② ≤ ⅓ of resale (all-in) ③ ≤ cheapest active listing
📞
Voicemail first. Let every seller call go to VM → reverse-lookup the number → pull the parcel → THEN call back informed. First call = questions only, never accept a price on call #1.
🏦
Owner-finance = the money engine. Buy $3.5k → sell $1k down + $500/mo × 48 ≈ $25k. ~1 in 5 default around month 4 → you keep every payment AND the land, then resell. Stack 4–5 notes ≈ $2k+/mo passive.

Luxury Teardowns — Lux Blueprint

Old house on premium dirt → developers. Pockets: Brentwood 37027 · Atlanta 30327 · Coastal OC
1

Filter Zillow

Built ≥ 2022 · houses · $2M+ · remove boundary. No $2M+ new builds nearby = kill it.

2

Find builder's cost

Open 3–4 new builds → price history → what they PAID for the raw lot. That's true land value.

3

Hunt the owners

Old small houses (≤25% of new-build sqft) whose Zestimate is $200–300k ABOVE builder cost.

4

MAO

Dispo price − $100–150k. Round DOWN to the number you can defend to a developer.

⚡ The 3× Rule (fast gut-check)

📐
Lock a teardown at ~$1M only if new builds in that pocket sell $3M+. Ratio not there? Walk. Works in most luxury markets.

🚫 The 3 Hard Rules

🌊 Water only comps water (never mix) 🛣️ Never cross a 3-lane / yellow-line road 📏 Value buildable area, not deed acreage

✍️ The Signing Move

"Our simple 4-page vs. the state contract?" DocuSign — walk page 1 only Never mention the earnest deposit
💬
The "politician" tactic. Seller asks a question you can't answer (lot size, lender)? Never answer straight — give a vague positive + fire back YOUR question. "It's a tennis match — you only score by serving the ball back. End everything with a question."
🧭

Golden Rules (all 3 courses agree)

Break these and you lose money. Follow them and it's hard to.

🔎 Research is the product
You get paid for knowing the zoning/price truth. Do the homework before you spend a dollar on mail.
🚫 Never blind-offer
All 3 teach conversation-first pricing. A formula lowballs your best deals and overpays your worst.
📬 Mail is king
Personal, handwritten-style, no "investor." Direct REI native. 10%+ response on a tight list.
🤝 Buyers first
Build the buyer list while you build the pull list. Copy every developer name as you research.
🧾 Round DOWN
Always take the safe number you can defend. "The worst thing is chasing your best comp and failing."
🗺️ Know your unit
Infill = ZIP. Rec = COUNTY. Luxury = POCKET. Never zoom out past your unit.
⚠️
Texas & other non-disclosure states (TX, NM, ID, UT, MT): Zillow hides sold prices — undercounts everything. Judge by new-construction counts + direct builder calls instead.
